Same stats, different data

Datasets can share their means, standard deviations, correlations, even their boxplots, and still look nothing alike. Summary statistics never tell the whole story.

"…make both calculations and graphs. Both sorts of output should be studied; each will contribute to understanding." F. J. Anscombe, 1973

Four datasets of 11 (x, y) pairs. Compare their statistics first: what do you expect the plots to look like? Then reveal them. Once revealed, drag any point to see how the statistics change.

Showing: Dinosaur

Five datasets of 2,484 values each. Their boxplots are identical. Switch views to see what the boxplots hide.
